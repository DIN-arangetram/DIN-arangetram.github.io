The Occasion

A Bharatanātyam Arangetram is a sacred milestone: the first public presentation of a disciple after years of dedicated training. This evening honors the tradition of Guru and Sishya, the lineage of classical dance, and the timeless stories brought to life through rhythm, gesture, and expression.

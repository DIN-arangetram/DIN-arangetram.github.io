Meet the Disciples

Dhruthi, Ishana, and Niveda—the students presenting their Arangetram.

Sishya – Dhruthi Allu

A rising ninth grader at Farragut High School.

Dhruthi Allu, a rising ninth grader at Farragut High School, is the daughter of Dr. Srikanth Allu and Mrs. Sowjanya Mandadapu and the sister of Dhaksh Allu. She began training in Bharatanatyam in 2015 under the guidance of her guru, Mrs. Bhavani Murthy. Through years of dedicated learning, Dhruthi has participated in numerous cultural programs and performances, developing grace, discipline, and a deep appreciation for this classical art form. She is grateful to her guru for her guidance, encouragement, and dedication in nurturing her passion for Bharatanatyam and shaping her journey as a dancer.

In addition to her commitment to dance, Dhruthi has excelled academically, consistently demonstrating dedication and a strong commitment to learning. She has been a vital member of her robotics team for the past three years and has competed at the national level, showcasing strong problem-solving skills, leadership, and teamwork. Her team has won state championships and participated in FLL international events. She has also excelled in the arts, with her work on a handcrafted Epiphone guitar selected for the prestigious Dogwood Arts Student Exhibition. Dhruthi is actively involved in several clubs and community service, including volunteering at pet shelters and participating in fundraising events, demonstrating compassion and a strong sense of responsibility. Outside of academics and extracurriculars, she enjoys piano recitals and playing tennis. She aspires to pursue a career in medicine and become a doctor.

Sishya – Ishana Singhvi

A rising sophomore at Farragut High School.

Ishana Singhvi, daughter of Vikas Singhvi and Yukti Sancheti, and sister of Vivaan Singhvi, is a dedicated Bharatanātyam dancer and a cherished member of Natana Manohari School of Performing Arts for over nine years under the tutelage of Guru Bhavani Murthy. Through disciplined training and devotion to this classical art form, she has grown as both a dancer and an individual, culminating in this special milestone…her Arangetram.

A rising sophomore at Farragut High School, Ishana is a hardworking student with a strong interest in STEM. She has earned recognition for her academic achievements and is actively involved in organizations such as DECA and HOSA, where she continues to build leadership and teamwork skills. Beyond dance and academics, Ishana enjoys art, design, jewelry making, and spending time with her beloved Cavapoo, Leo. Her warmth, creativity, determination, and joyful spirit shine through in all she does. Today, as she presents her Arangetram, Ishana celebrates years of dedication and artistic growth. She is deeply grateful to her guru, family, friends, and everyone who has supported her along the way.

Sishya – Niveda Krishna

A rising ninth grader at Farragut High School.

Niveda Krishna, daughter of Manu Gopalakrishnan and Biji Krishna, is a rising 9th grader at Farragut High School. She began her Bharatanatyam training at the age of five under the expert guidance of Guru Smt. Bhavani Murthy at the Natana Manohari School of Dance. Over the years, Niveda has developed a deep passion and profound respect for this classical dance form. For her, Bharatanatyam is much more than a dance discipline; it serves as a meaningful connection to her Indian roots, culture, and tradition. During her years of training, she has formed lasting friendships with her fellow dancers, making this journey even more rewarding.

Niveda loves staying active and creative. She plays both piano and violin, and has participated in piano Guild performances, Junior Clinic, and Chamber orchestra. She enjoys playing tennis and exploring her interest in STEM through her robotics club, which has won state championships and participates in international competitions. At school, Niveda keeps busy with her academics as well as after-school activities like Mathcounts, News crew, and Student Council. Through the Student Council, she helped organize fundraisers and volunteered at animal shelters and nature centers. As Niveda presents her Arangetram today, she would like to extend her heartfelt gratitude to her guru, family, friends, and the Knoxville community for their unwavering support throughout her artistic journey.

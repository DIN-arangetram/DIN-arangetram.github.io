Sishya – Dhruthi Allu

A rising ninth grader at Farragut High School.

Dhruthi Allu began training in Bharatanatyam in 2015 under the guidance of her guru, Mrs. Bhavani Murthy. Through years of dedicated learning, she has participated in numerous cultural programs, developing grace, discipline, and a deep appreciation for this classical art form.

Beyond dance, Dhruthi excels academically and has been a vital member of her robotics team with state championships and national-level participation. She also created art selected for the Dogwood Arts Student Exhibition, volunteers in her community, enjoys piano and tennis, and aspires to pursue medicine.

Sishya – Ishana Singhvi

A rising sophomore at Farragut High School.

Ishana Singhvi has been a dedicated Bharatanātyam dancer and a cherished member of Natana Manohari School of Performing Arts for over nine years under Guru Bhavani Murthy. Her disciplined training has helped her grow as both a dancer and an individual.

Ishana is active in STEM organizations such as DECA and HOSA, and she enjoys art, jewelry making, and spending time with her Cavapoo, Leo. Her warm and joyful spirit accompanies her devotion to dance and academics.

Sishya – Niveda Krishna

A rising ninth grader at Farragut High School.

Niveda Krishna began Bharatanatyam training at age five with Guru Bhavani Murthy. Over the years, she has developed a deep passion for dance, embracing it as a meaningful connection to her Indian roots, culture, and tradition.

Niveda is active in music, robotics, Student Council, and community service. She plays piano and violin, participates in Mathcounts, news crew, and has helped organize fundraisers. She thanks her guru, family, and community for their support on this special stage debut.

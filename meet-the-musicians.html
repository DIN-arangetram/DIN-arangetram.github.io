Collective Impact: Together, this team has presented Arangetrams, concerts, workshops, and recordings across the world. Their music is loved for its blend of classical depth, soulful Carnatic ragas, and rhythmic energy, creating unique experiences for dancers and audiences alike.

Shri Nandakumar Unnikrishnan (Vocals)

A renowned Carnatic vocalist, Nandakumar, has accompanied over 600 Arangetrams worldwide. Trained under Sri Kalarcode Mahadevan and Sri Chandramana Narayanan Namboothiri, he is admired for his melodious voice, musical sensitivity, and strong support for the performer. He has toured extensively across India, the USA, Europe, Australia, and more, receiving honors including the Aryabhatta Excellence Award, Sangeetha Shreshta, and many other recognitions.

Ravi Kulur (Flute)

An internationally acclaimed flautist, Ravi Kulur, was trained under his father, Sri K. Raghavendra Rao. Pandit Ravi Shankar praised him as a “flute maestro.” Ravi has performed over 1200 concerts in 34 countries at prestigious venues including Carnegie Hall, Kennedy Center, Sydney Opera House, Boston Symphony Hall, and many more.

His music bridges Carnatic classical, world music, and fusion jazz, with collaborations featuring Ravi Shankar, Anoushka Shankar, AR Rahman, Ricky Kej, and many others. Through his academy, Ravi has trained students across India and abroad, while his organization actively nurtures and promotes young talent in classical instrumental music.

Karthik Vydhatri (Percussion)

A versatile percussionist, Karthik trained under his father Vidwan S. V. Giridhar in Carnatic percussion and Vid Arun Kumar in electronic rhythm pads. Skilled in Mridangam, Rhythm Pads, Morsing, and Kanjira, he is known for blending traditional and contemporary styles with great versatility.

He regularly performs at various Carnatic sabhas and dance festivals across India and internationally, and is currently pursuing advanced training under renowned Mridangam maestro Trichy B. Harikumar.
